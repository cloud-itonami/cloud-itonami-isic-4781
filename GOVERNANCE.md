# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-4781 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Sales-record (inventory/sale/return) logging, stall
placement/staffing scheduling proposals, supply-order coordination, and
compliance-concern flagging (suspected food-safety-clearance failure,
age-verification failure for tobacco sales, or stall/market-permit
lapse) for mobile/temporary market-stall and street-market vendors
selling food, beverages, and tobacco products.

Out-of-scope: Finalizing a food-safety clearance, finalizing an
age-verification override (tobacco sales), direct point-of-sale
age-verification/ID hardware actuation, and market/health/
tobacco-licensing-authority enforcement (stall/market permit issuance/
suspension, health-department enforcement, compliance enforcement).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
